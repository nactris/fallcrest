Stern looking, middle aged man. Has a long scar from chin to the top of his face. 
Does not accept any food or drink offered in [[Moonstone Keep\|Moonstone Keep]].

He is the reason the [[Places/Fallcrest\|Fallcrest]] almost became a hellhole. 
Currently plotting a downfall of [[Lord Warden Aurelio Markelhay \|Lord Warden]].
He is the boss of [[Characters/Lucian Grimhollow\|Lucian Grimhollow]] but Lucian dislikes his methods.

The crusaders are building gallows and are capturing people into the dungeon. 
Earlier they were dragging people out of their homes.
