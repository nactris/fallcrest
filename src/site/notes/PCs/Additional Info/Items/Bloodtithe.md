Dark metal sword with demonic inscriptions. Rightfully taken from [[Characters/Fallcrest/Lucian Grimhollow\|Lucian Grimhollow]] by [[PCs/Zimriel Kachaçe-Queensward\|Zim]] after his defeat. (they both bet their weapons on winning). Extremely irky about [[Nathaniel\|Nathaniel]], almost killed him and was promised [[Characters/Fallcrest/Commander Toll\|Toll's]] heart instead.


> [!error]- Bloodtithe
> Each battle while staggered. when you first take damage that as a type (acid, cold, fire, etc.), you deal [2] damage of that type with weapon attacks for the rest of the battle (champion: +2d6; epic: +3d6).

> [!warning]- Curse
> When you roll a natural 1 (on any d20 roll), you take double the weapon's extra damage. 

> [!Info] Quirk
> The grass is always greener. (Your regrets and lusts are amplified)
