Given by [[Characters/Fallcrest/Lord Warden Aurelio Markelhay\|Lord Warden Aurelio Markelhay]] as a reward.

> [!ERROR]- Sherif's Ring of Knocking
> PD +1
> (move action - recharge 6+)
> 
The ring was owned by a famously unpopular tax collector. As a move action, make a skill check to break, knock down, or damage a non-magical object you are next to. Use a +15 bonus to your roll instead of your ability modifier + level + background (epic: +20).

> [!INFO]- Quirk
> Executes duties without compassion.
