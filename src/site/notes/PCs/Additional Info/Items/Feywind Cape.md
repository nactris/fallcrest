Pale-green cape similar in shape to joined together dragonfly wings. When used it separates into functional  wings and gives of green glow. Flying with them is pretty hard on the user. Obtained by [[PCs/Mira Rosalis\|Mira]] at the end of [[Places/Dungeons/The Gallantry of Britobart\|The Gallantry of Britobart]].


> [!ERROR]- Feywind Cape
> PD +1
> (move action – recharge 6+ after use)
> 
> Sprout fey wings and Fly in a colorful whirl, descending to the ground at the start of your next turn. If you fail the recharge roll, you lose a recovery (and your lunch!). If you succeed, you regain the power and can use it again before descending without the failure effect.

> [!INFO]- Quirk
> A flair for the dramatic in battle.
