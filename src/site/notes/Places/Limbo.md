One of several Incredibly chaotic "places" outside of reality. A connective tissue spanning between planes. [[Characters/Fallcrest/Lady Allande\|Lady Allande's]] soul was trapped in the "region" between Hells and Material Plane. Limbo usually refers to regions between Material Plane and places where souls go after death, different "regions" can have diferent names.
[[PCs/Mira Rosalis\|Mira's]] plane-hopping powers were wildly amplified in there. She usually visits different region.

[[Places/Demiplanes\|Demiplanes]] exists as separated "cubes" within limbo.
